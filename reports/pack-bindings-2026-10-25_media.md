# A review trail for historical package quantities

Herman can now store whether a past package quantity has a supported conversion or needs human review. A late conversion does not silently rewrite that stored check. An owner or reviewer can add an evidenced decision, keeping the original record and earlier decisions visible.

The storage and related inventory/import checks passed 68 local database tests. The live analysis connection is the next step, so this is not yet a complete released correction workflow. GitHub publication and full CI are pending after permission-review timeouts.

Pricing is also recorded in the dashboard plan: transparent product costs, price scenarios, competitor/customer evidence and product/activity contribution. Those screens and calculations are planned, not already available.
