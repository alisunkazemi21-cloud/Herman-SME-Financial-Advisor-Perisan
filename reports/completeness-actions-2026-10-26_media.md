# What the completeness and follow-up workflow adds

Herman is gaining a clearer answer to two practical questions: have the necessary records been supplied, and who will investigate the result?

A manager can review each category of records, including an explicit statement when there were no returns, waste or similar events. Missing records remain visible. When the data changes, the earlier completeness review needs attention. Conditional future-period calculations stay in their original receipts and are kept out of current business figures.

An investigation can have a named responsible person, follow-up date, human approval and an evidence-backed outcome. Unsuccessful and inconclusive work remains part of the history. A stock difference still means something needs investigation; it does not establish a cause or prove a saving.
