# Business records can now become reviewed journal entries

The backend can record a transaction across several accounts, attach its source evidence, and wait for a human reviewer before including it in balances. It checks that debit and credit totals agree and flags matching posting patterns for duplicate review.

An approved mistake can be corrected through a separately reviewed reversal while keeping the original entry visible. A dated trial balance shows opening balances, movements during the period and closing balances using exact numbers.

This supplies the accounting foundation for future advisor and portfolio work. It does not automatically classify statutory statements or replace a reviewer's accounting judgment. The visible interface remains scheduled for later work; validation details are recorded in the checkpoint report.
