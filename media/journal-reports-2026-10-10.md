# Reviewed journals can now inform the business advisor

The backend can turn approved journal entries into a saved business report using account classifications reviewed by a person. The report supplies cash movement, five financial indicators and a link back to the exact records and documents used.

A later backdated posting marks the old report as stale. A reviewer can approve a linked replacement while keeping the earlier receipt available. The advisor and portfolio avoid presenting stale or conflicting reports as current numbers.

Cash totals describe net movement in each journal, so they do not replace detailed bank records. Reviewers still need to assess the reporting period and completeness. Validation uses synthetic data; the visible interface and real-model evaluation remain later work.
