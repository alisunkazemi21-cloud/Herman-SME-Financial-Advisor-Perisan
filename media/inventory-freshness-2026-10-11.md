# When an earlier inventory answer needs a new calculation

An inventory result can be accurate for the information it used and still be outdated after a correction. C14 keeps the earlier answer and its evidence intact, then separately checks whether its recorded inputs still match the business records.

If inputs have changed, portfolio and advisor data identify the affected records and ask for a new calculation after counts and coverage are reviewed. They do not present the earlier shortage as a current finding. If the comparison cannot be completed, the status is unknown. An unchanged result may still have missing information; matching inputs are not a new certification of the original documents.

The final suite passed **313 tests**, with **one real-OCR skip**, including **36 freshness checks**; [the run record](../runs/inventory-freshness-2026-10-11/README.md) contains the software evidence. This is backend behavior using synthetic tests. The visible Persian portfolio and real-engine/operational acceptance remain future work.
