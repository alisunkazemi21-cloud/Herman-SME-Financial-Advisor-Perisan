# Correct business knowledge while keeping the earlier record

When a business statement is wrong or outdated, a user can propose an evidenced replacement linked to the original. A reviewer can read both claims before approving or rejecting the change. Until approval, the existing claim remains current.

After approval, fresh advisor context and portfolio highlights use the corrected claim. The original remains in history, and earlier conversations retain their exact context. A correction replaces the whole claim, including its dates; it does not erase unrelated conflicting evidence or change financial figures.
