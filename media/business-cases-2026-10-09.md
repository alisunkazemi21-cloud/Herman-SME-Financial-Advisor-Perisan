# A business question can now keep its history

The backend can save a financial case and continue it with numbered questions and replies. Each answer keeps the information used to produce it, so a future screen can show both the conversation and its supporting context.

Recent replies help the advisor follow the discussion, but they do not become approved financial facts. Existing review controls still determine which business records supply calculations. Quick remains a separate temporary conversation mode without saved history.
