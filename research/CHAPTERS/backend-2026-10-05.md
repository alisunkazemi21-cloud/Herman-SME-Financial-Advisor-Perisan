# From a single-user file to a business profile

Checkpoint: `backend-2026-10-05`.

Business records are independent of conversation: original hashed documents, proposals, human decisions and analysis snapshots have separate structures. Consumption derives from fulfilled events and the recipe version valid at their time. Missing opening/closing counts, incomplete coverage, overlapping recipes or invalid unit conversion produce an incomplete result instead of a guessed shortage.

Isolation was tested through service membership and PostgreSQL RLS under a non-owner role. Composite foreign keys prevent cross-business item references. A synthetic benchmark with 1,000 businesses and one million movements exposed repeated per-row membership checks; migration 2 evaluates membership once per statement while preserving tenant scope.

The before/after timings are not a controlled cold-cache experiment. See [acceptance evidence](../BACKEND_ACCEPTANCE.md). At this checkpoint, language-model evaluation, document queues and conversational memory remained unimplemented; later checkpoints address some of that work.
