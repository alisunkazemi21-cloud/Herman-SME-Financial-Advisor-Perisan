# From recorded inputs to reviewed completeness

A mathematically correct reconciliation can still be operationally misleading when returns, waste, staff meals or stock counts were never supplied. C17 therefore separates a calculation's original assumptions from a reviewed assessment of twelve evidence categories for an exact warehouse, period and calculation basis. An empty table is not an assertion that nothing happened.

The review is bound to a deterministic, server-derived dossier of active operational facts, approvals and correction identities. Counts at both boundaries and recipe lines are included. Later recorded changes invalidate the support of the earlier review; C16 separately governs labels and pack interpretation. Forced tenant policies, composite references and append-only guards preserve the source and review history.

An approved assessment may truthfully report missing evidence and still withhold a numeric result. A review made before the period has ended remains provisional, irrespective of subsequent clock passage. The raw conditional receipt is preserved while current advisor and portfolio figures are withheld. Pre-C17 receipts remain readable but lack the new operational-completeness evidence required for current advice.

Investigation actions are a separate reviewed history. A responsible person and follow-up date make a proposed task accountable; sequenced events record its review, work and outcome. Confirmed, ineffective and inconclusive outcomes are retained. Neither a detected variance nor an outcome label establishes theft, causality or verified savings.

The [contract](../COMPLETENESS_ACTIONS.md) and [run record](../../runs/completeness-actions-2026-10-26/README.md) distinguish implementation and synthetic validation from the remaining pilot, recovery, engine-quality and capacity gates. This is backend work; the Persian owner workflow remains a later acceptance step.
