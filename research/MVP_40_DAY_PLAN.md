# Herman: forty-day MVP delivery plan

Planning baseline: **2026-10-24**. Day 1 is October 24 and Day 40 is **December 2, 2026**, inclusive, in Asia/Tehran. These are target dates, not a delivery guarantee or evidence of completion.

The founder is available full-time and can approach a restaurant/cafe this week. A willing pilot, permissioned records and a reviewer are **not yet confirmed**. Engineering and customer preparation run together; waiting until the application is finished to recruit a pilot would leave too little time to test recurring value.

## Outcome and current position

The target is one usable, supported workflow: business access → evidenced upload → explicit import mapping → duplicate and completeness review → inventory reconciliation → reviewed investigation/action → follow-up. Begin with a few expensive ingredients at a restaurant/cafe that already keeps usable sales, recipe and stock records. A discrepancy is an investigation prompt, not proof of theft or a verified saving.

The existing backend also supplies business knowledge, case history, journal reports, cash-flow data, five indicators and stateless Quick calculations/document intake. Reuse those capabilities; do not rebuild them. A minimal Persian owner screen is proposed **after the backend gates**: business selection, intake/review, reconciliation, portfolio and knowledge, plus the separate Quick path. Financial cards must show missing/stale states when their required reviewed inputs are absent. Inventory-only pilot records cannot populate financial indicators by assumption.

**Current status:** C15 is the last fully verified checkpoint. C16 is in progress: dated pack calculation, internal revision persistence and analysis/freshness/duplicate integration have scoped passing evidence. Historical record bindings, item/SKU revision lifecycle and public catalog routes remain open. No pilot, real-model quality, restore or full application capacity gate is closed by this plan. [Progress and evidence](PROGRESS.md) remain the authoritative implementation record.

## Responsibilities and working rhythm

**Founder:** customer recruitment and permission, sample preparation with the business, acceptance of Persian wording, manual baseline/outcome verification, deployment budget/access decisions and real customer communication. Full-time availability makes these tasks possible; it does not establish confirmed customer access or accounting expertise. Obtain accounting review where a claim depends on it.

**Engineering assistant and explicitly delegated specialists:** inspect the existing system, document contracts before code, implement bounded tasks, validate changes, maintain the evidence trail and make reviewable commits. Specialists own separate files or modules, hand off tests and limitations, and receive independent integration review. Installed role descriptions are working guidance, not evidence of completed work or autonomous staffing.

During active work, maintain one current implementation gate and a short blocker list. At each checkpoint record the code revision, tests, failed checks and repairs, remaining gate and next task in the maintained progress map and synchronized artifacts. Use factual states: planned, in progress, verified or gate open. Never substitute an overall completion percentage for evidence. Weekly founder reviews use the pilot period records and actual time/cost logs. No scheduled background execution or customer messages are created by this plan.

New requests enter the backlog unless they remove a release blocker. After three unsuccessful attempts at the same bounded task, stop repeating the approach, record the evidence and change the implementation strategy or scope. Do not advance a failed dependency merely to match the calendar.

## Scope retained beyond the first release

The broad financial-advisor objective remains: varied financial documents and businesses, deeper accounting/report controls, more integrations, reliable model interpretation and richer decision support. The first release does not promise universal document understanding, autonomous financial posting, accounting-standard compliance or proven economics. Broad POS connectors, extra sectors and additional forecasting/benchmark features remain backlog work. Existing features stay subject to their documented input requirements.

Keep documentation in English and app/data in Persian. At the later UI checkpoint inspect the project and Contour package before using the requested `npx vibefarsi add contour`. Prefer Kalameh only when legitimately available; retain the existing fallback while licensed webfont files are unavailable. Backend validation remains the first priority.
