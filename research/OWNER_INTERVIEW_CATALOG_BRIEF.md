# Queued after C17: Persian owner interview catalog

Requested by the founder on 2026-10-26. Begin the deliverable after the current engineering checkpoint; this brief does not replace C17 acceptance.

Prepare a friendly Persian catalog/interview pack for cafe and restaurant owners. It has two purposes: gather market/customer evidence for market analysis and product R&D, and invite voluntary sharing of a small permissioned sample of financial/operational records. The founder wants an approachable, motivating explanation that makes participation worthwhile.

Planned artifacts: a printable Persian PDF and editable source, a short interviewer guide, structured response fields and an optional sample-data checklist/permission form. Ask about existing routines, painful investigations, record availability, decision makers, current tools, setup/support burden, willingness to pilot/pay and concrete examples. Distinguish observed behavior from opinions and hypothetical prices. Preserve refusals and missing records in research rather than counting only positive interviews.

Explain Herman's current evidence/review workflow in plain Persian and distinguish delivered features from future plans. Offer a useful, feasible proposed exchange such as a reviewed sample findings discussion, subject to agreement, without promising savings, security certifications or capabilities not yet demonstrated. Owners may skip questions or decline data sharing without losing the interview opportunity.

The founder explicitly approved an incentive: one month of free Herman platform membership plus one month of free gym membership. Final naming direction: no city or gym name is to appear in the catalog; no provider has been selected. Persian offer: «در صورت همکاری، از یک ماه اشتراک رایگان هرمان و یک ماه عضویت رایگان باشگاه ورزشی بهره‌مند خواهید شد.» Activation timing and usage conditions will be agreed with each participant before cooperation/sample transfer; do not invent a signed gym partnership. Keep optional model-training permission independent rather than silently making every consent box a condition of the incentive.

Request minimal sample records relevant to an agreed question: e.g. short-period sales export, purchases, opening/closing stock counts and selected recipes, with explicit units and dates. Give practical redaction instructions for customer/employee identities, bank/account details and unrelated confidential material. Record source, period, permission and allowed use; do not request credentials or unrestricted system access. Identify a safe agreed transfer method before collecting actual files.

The founder confirmed separate opt-in choices for product research, OCR evaluation and model training. Do not silently equate product testing with consent to train a model. The final catalog needs independent choices for those uses, sharing/processing, retention/deletion and a contact; agree those practices before making promises in the consent text. No customer outreach or actual collection is authorized by preparing the pack.

Founder contact already supplied for this project: Alireza Kazemi. Confirm the desired public contact details when producing the final artifact; do not copy credentials or private developer metadata into it. The catalog itself must be Persian under this explicit user request. Developer/research notes remain English.
